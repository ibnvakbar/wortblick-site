Understand every German word as you read.

Wortblick is a free browser extension for people learning German. Hold Shift and point at any German word on any website: a small card shows its article, plural, English meanings and an example sentence.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

What it does

  • Knows German grammar. Inflected words lead back to their base word: “Häusern” shows das Haus, “ging” shows gehen.
  • Understands compound nouns. “Krankenversicherungskarte” is split into Kranken + Versicherung + Karte, with its article: die.
  • Der, die or das at a glance. Every noun shows its article and plural.
  • Save words in context. One click saves the word with the sentence you found it in.
  • Works offline. The whole dictionary comes with the extension.
  • Private by design. No account, no tracking, no analytics. Nothing you read or save leaves your browser. Privacy policy
